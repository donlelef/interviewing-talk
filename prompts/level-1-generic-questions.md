# Level 1 Prompt: Generic Questions

Copy and paste this prompt into Cursor.

```text
I want to build an app for companies that offer an employee psychological support desk.

Before writing code, ask me questions to understand the app better.

The app may involve employees, HR or company admins, therapists, possible case managers, appointment booking, consent, aggregate metrics, privacy, and urgent or high-risk situations.

Do not write code yet. Just ask questions.
```

## What This Level Demonstrates

This prompt is intentionally weak.

It usually produces a long list of broad questions. That is useful as a starting point, but it often mixes product, compliance, UX, and technical architecture without helping the product owner decide what to answer first.
