# Q&A Board

The language of a live session where the audience asks the speaker questions.

## Language

**Speaker**:
The person presenting the session, who answers Questions out loud.
_Avoid_: Host, presenter, admin

**Attendee**:
A person in the audience, recognised by their browser, never by an account or a name.
_Avoid_: User, participant, voter, guest

**Question**:
Something an Attendee asks the Speaker, at most 280 characters long.
_Avoid_: Post, message, comment

**Upvote**:
An Attendee's signal that they want a Question answered; an Attendee gives a Question at most one, and can take it back.
_Avoid_: Vote, like, +1
